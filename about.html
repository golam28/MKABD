About the firm

Four decades of legal excellence

A Dhaka chamber built on Supreme Court advocacy and professional ethics since 1986.

Dr. M. Khaled Ahmed
Founding Partner & Senior Advocate, Supreme Court of Bangladesh
Former Senior Vice President, Supreme Court Bar Association of Bangladesh

M. Khaled Ahmed & Associates has practised in Bangladesh since 1986. The firm was founded on professional ethics and effective legal solutions, and has grown into a full-service practice in Dhaka — civil, criminal, commercial, and corporate work.

Every client is entitled to thorough preparation, honest counsel, and vigorous advocacy. Whether the matter is a constitutional writ before the Supreme Court, a commercial dispute, or a family proceeding, the same standard of care applies.

Instructions are accepted in the name of the chamber. Conduct is assigned according to the brief; the founding senior advocate retains overall responsibility for the standard of the set.

Jurisdiction

Where we appear

Counsel of the chamber hold standing before both Divisions of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh. A dedicated court chamber is maintained at Chamber 3016, Annexe Building. We also appear in the civil and criminal courts, tax tribunals, and in arbitration as the brief requires.
