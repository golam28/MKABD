M. Khaled Ahmed & Associates has practised in Bangladesh since 1986. The firm was founded on professional ethics and effective legal solutions, and has grown into a full-service practice in Dhaka — civil, criminal, commercial, and corporate work.

Every client is entitled to thorough preparation, honest counsel, and vigorous advocacy. Whether the matter is a constitutional writ before the Supreme Court, a commercial dispute, or a family proceeding, the same standard of care applies.

Founding Senior Advocate

Dr. M. Khaled Ahmed

Senior Advocate, Supreme Court of Bangladesh. Former Senior Vice President, Supreme Court Bar Association of Bangladesh.

Dr. M. Khaled Ahmed founded the chambers in Dhaka in 1986, with Supreme Court advocacy and professional ethics as the foundation of the practice. He is a Senior Advocate of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh and a former Senior Vice President of the Supreme Court Bar Association of Bangladesh.

The set grew from a court chamber into a full-service practice: civil and criminal litigation, writs, company and commercial work, tax, banking, family, immigration, admiralty, and arbitration. He retains overall conduct. Associates prepare, appear, and advise within the standard of the chamber.

  • Supreme Court appearances Hearings in the High Court Division and the Appellate Division, including leave, stay, and final relief.
  • Conduct of the chamber Instructions are accepted in the name of the firm. An associate is assigned according to the brief; the senior advocate retains conduct where required.
  • Opinions and strategy Written and oral advice on whether to sue, defend, petition, settle, or arbitrate — including advice not to proceed.
  • Bar leadership Service as Senior Vice President of the Supreme Court Bar Association informs the chamber’s standard of etiquette before the Court.

Remote practice · Electronic Immigration Network

UK immigration support from Bangladesh

The chamber accepts remote instructions on immigration-related matters connected with Bangladesh and the wider region. Work is carried out from Dhaka and may be instructed through the Electronic Immigration Network (EIN), or directly by solicitors, counsel, and regulated advisers in the United Kingdom.

Typical instructions include independent country expert reports, verification of Bangladesh court and government records, certified document trails, and Bangladesh-side evidence packages to support applications, appeals, and judicial review. We do not hold ourselves out as Immigration Advice Authority (IAA) regulated advisers for the giving of UK immigration advice; where full UK application representation is required, we work alongside your regulated UK adviser or solicitor.

  • Country expert reports Situation and risk reports for Bangladesh and related jurisdictions, suitable for use by instructing UK counsel in asylum, human rights, and protection claims.
  • Document & record verification Checking of judgments, GDs, case records, and other official papers from Bangladesh courts, tribunals, and public offices (where access is lawful).
  • Evidence packages Structured bundles for UK practitioners: translations coordination, certified copies, and chronological evidence notes from the Bangladesh side.
  • Remote conferences Video conferences with instructing solicitors, counsel, or the client’s UK team; written opinions on Bangladesh law and practice.
